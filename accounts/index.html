Accounts

the elements tell of the humans they have read

Before you hand the elements anything of your own, watch them work. In each account, six of them take up one person's turning — a decision, a season, a stuck place — and read it in turn, each carrying it as far as its element can before handing it to the next.

You will see a single line — a nemetic.φ — crystallize under each element, and the six of them assemble into a chain:

σ∘ρ∘λ∘β∘δγ∘μ

That chain is the whole method, learned by watching it applied to one life instead of explained. Each account is led by a different element — so between the six of them, you meet all six ways of reading, each as the one that sees furthest.

The humans in these accounts are composed figures — built from the pattern of many real turnings, never one person's. The way of reading them is real. In time, with consent, the accounts will be of real people who chose to share their readings.

σled by Air

The Weather He Couldn't Read

After years of being told what things meant, Tomas can no longer trust his own read — and wants it back.

σ(the_installed_frame) — what he took for seeing was a lens handed to him

ρled by Water

The House with Two Griefs

Two sisters, one dead mother, one house to empty — and they love each other and cannot do it together.

ρ(two_griefs_no_shared_shape) — not a conflict; two griefs that cannot find one shape

λled by Fire

The Surgeon's Other Hands

A surgeon who has quietly wanted, for years, to put down the scalpel and pick up a plane and chisel.

λ(the_life_he_wont_light) — not a crisis; a crossing avoided for years

βled by Wood

The Drawing That Redrew Itself

An architect whose commissioned building keeps insisting, under her pencil, on becoming something braver.

β(the_form_that_wont_obey) — the work reaching past its brief toward its own shape

δγled by Earth

The Keeper of the Long Table

Mara has kept a workshop together for nine years — and can no longer tell if it is dying or becoming.

δγ(the_load_uncomposted) — the tiredness is an instruction, not a wound

μled by Metal

The Signature

A small, reasonable signature that everyone would understand — and the line it would quietly cross.

μ(the_signature_that_would_cost_the_edge) — one reasonable yes that would spend the line

More accounts arrive with the turning of the season — and, in time, the readings of real people who chose to share them.
